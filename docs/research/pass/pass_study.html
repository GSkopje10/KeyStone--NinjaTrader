Going forward: the rotation used only to pass evaluations

  • Expect about 10 passes per 50 evaluations with the chosen set-up (19.1% on 2024–26), i.e. about $639 of evaluation fees per passed account. A batch of 50 is finished (all passed or blown) after about 37 trading days.
  • Day plan: FULL RECOVERY passed the most in 2024–26 (11.5% pooled, vs ONE TRADE A DAY 11.3%, UNTIL THE DAY TARGET 10.6%).
  • Target / stop: +$1,500 / −$1,000 passed the most (18.0%); +$1,500 / −$500 10.5%, +$900 / −$500 5.2%, +$900 / −$1,000 11.0%.
  • Session: LONDON 03:00–07:00 passed the most (13.0%); the weakest was NY OPEN 09:30–11:00 (9.0%).
  • Sides: the best pooled side choice in 2024–26 was FADE THE LAST 5 MIN (BOTH AGREE) (12.8%) against random sides at 10.5% — within luck: pick the sides any way you like (random is as good) and spend the effort on the plan, the target / stop and the session.
  • Same direction vs hedged: both legs the same way (BUY-BUY / SELL-SELL) passed 12.1% in 2024–26, hedged (BUY MNQ + SELL MGC / the reverse) 9.1%. MNQ and MGC often move together, so a hedged pair moves less and reaches +$1,500 less often before the stop — keep both legs the same way; which way (buy or sell) is close to a coin.
  • Firms: rotating A → B → C → D → E keeps every firm at one account in five; the pass rate per firm is the same within luck (same rules) — see FIRMS. Use it to spread the activity, not as an edge.
  • Set-up to start with: FULL RECOVERY • +$1,500 / −$1,000 • FADE THE LAST 5 MIN (BOTH AGREE) • LONDON 03:00–07:00 — 2020–23 22.5%, 2024–26 20.9% (177 of 848).
  • Keep honest: the 2024–26 numbers are the ones to trust (the set-ups were not chosen on them). Results on history are not a promise; trade it small first and compare your real pass rate with these.

How it was measured

  • Real 1-minute MNQ and MGC bars, 2020-10-19 → 2026-10-02 (1,402 days with both). The app's own engine (the PROP SIM's rotation): one trade at a time, the account in turn, MNQ and MGC together with one combined $ target and stop, the stop checked first with both legs at their worst of the minute, targets at a minute's close, commission + 1 tick per contract and side.
  • Firm rules for every evaluation: LUCID FLEX 50K • evaluation $120 → +$3,000 (drawdown $2,000 EOD, best day ≤ 50%, ≥ 2 days) • funded: drawdown $2,000 EOD • payout after 5 day(s) of ≥ $150: 50% up to $2,000, you keep 90%, max 5 • 11-second rule • max 40 micros • flat by 16:45. An evaluation passes at the target with the consistency rule and the minimum days; it blows at the trailing drawdown. An account too close to its floor to carry a trade is counted as failed.
  • Batches: 50 evaluations bought on the batch's first day (50 × $120); none bought until every one has passed or blown; then a new batch starts the next trading day. Funded accounts are not traded or counted.
  • Firm rotation: account 1 = firm A, 2 = B, 3 = C, 4 = D, 5 = E, 6 = A …, and the rotation takes them in that order, so the same firm trades every fifth turn.
  • Every combination of 3 day plans × 4 targets / stops × 13 side rules × 6 sessions (936 set-ups) was run on 2020-10 → 2023-12 and, separately, on 2024-01 → 2026-10-02. The set-up was chosen on the first period only; the second period is the honest test. Random sides on 30 seeds give the luck band.
  • History is not a promise: fills, slippage on fast days, firm rule details and news days can make real results worse.
