1 • Why winning 9 days out of 10 can still lose money

One win = +$150. One loss = −$2,000. One loss erases 13 wins (2,000 ÷ 150 = 13.3). To break even you must win 2,000 ÷ 2,150 = 93.0% of the days — not 90%. Every 1% below 93% costs about $21 per trade. With a $1,000 stop you need 87.0%; with a $500 stop, 76.9%.
