Break of High: 09:30 or 10:00?

I built your setup as an exact rule and ran it on every MNQ day from October 2020 to October 2026. That's 1,536 versions on the 5-minute chart and 648 on the 1-minute chart, each run from both start times. 09:30 beats 10:00 clearly. But the rule taken on every setup has no edge in 2024–2026. The edge in your screenshots comes from which BH you choose, and that part isn't in the rule yet.

The answer

  • 09:30 wins the comparison. In 1,217 of the 1,536 rule versions, starting at 09:30 made more than starting at 10:00. On average a version made $2,571 more over the 6 years.
  • The plain rule is about break-even. First BH after 09:30, stop under the pattern, 2R target: +$0.90 a trade per micro over 1,460 trades. It made money every year 2021–2025, then lost $4,786 in 2026. The same rule from 10:00 lost $2.60 a trade.
  • It does not hold on 2024–2026. Only 153 of 1,536 versions from 09:30 (10%) and 76 from 10:00 (5%) were positive on 2024–26, against 991 and 683 on 2020–23. Of everything I added, only "swept the overnight low" made the 2020–23 numbers better, and it still ended flat on 2024–26.
  • 1-minute BH scalps lose. Only 11 of 648 versions from 09:30 were positive on 2024–26, averaging −$5.90 a trade. Costs eat 1-minute trades.
  • Your screenshots tell a different story from the rule. On the days I could match, you often skip the first BH and take a later one after the sell-off has finished. That choice is the part to capture next (see "What I need from you").
The rule I tested (read from your 72 screenshots)

How the BH was built

  1. On MNQ 5-minute candles from the start time (09:30 or 10:00): a red candle, then a green reference candle.
  2. The very next candle trades one tick above the reference high: a buy stop there. If the next candle doesn't break the high, the pattern is dropped and the search goes on.
  3. Stop one tick under the low of the red + green candles (or fixed 20 / 40 points). Targets tested: 1R, 2R, 3R, 30, 50 and 100 points, or hold to 15:55.
  4. Filters tested: a pull-back under the 09:30 open, a drop from the day's high of at least 0.1, 0.2 or 0.3 of the 14-day range, a sweep of the overnight low, a big red candle, two red candles in a row, first setup only or up to 3 a day, and last entry at 11:30 or 15:00.
  5. Costs: $1.50 commission and 1 tick of slippage per side. If the stop and target are hit in the same minute, the stop counts first.

Your screenshots against the rule

The rule takes the first BH. You often don't.

On 7 screenshot days inside the data, the first BH after 09:30 won once (+$458 on 8 Aug 2024), made $68 once and lost five times (−$49 to −$162). Where the chart times line up, your arrow is often on a later BH, after the sell-off finished. On 21 Jul 2026 the rule's 10:00 entry stopped out in 3 minutes, while your entry at about 10:20 matches the 10:00-start version that hit its 2R target (+$299). On 12 Dec 2025 your first arrow (≈10:35) lost too, matching the rule's 10:37 entry (−$162). Your second arrow at ≈12:05 was the winner.

So the screenshots show skill at picking the BH that comes after the selling is done. A mechanical "first BH" rule doesn't do that. Screenshots also mostly show trades that worked, so they can't measure the skipped and losing ones.
