Verdict

Can hedging make +$20 iterations low-risk?

The math

Zone recovery for a $2.00 target, worked out exactly

Buy 0.1 lot. If price falls Z, sell enough lots that the whole basket is +$20 once price runs another $2.00. If price comes back to the entry, buy enough to be +$20 at $2.00 above, and so on. Each leg survives only if price runs $2.00 before it travels the zone back, which happens with probability 2 / (Z + 2) when price has no direction. The last leg's failure costs everything the wins made. Costs are left out here; they only make it worse.

wins × $20 ≈ P(fail) × loss → expectancy = 0 − costs, for any hedge plan

This is the optional stopping theorem: if price has no direction, no plan of adding, hedging or closing can change the average. A hedge only changes the shape: more small wins, one rare large loss. The only way to get a positive average is an entry that reaches +$2.00 more often than chance.

Method

How it was run

  • Data: 1-minute gold futures (MGC) as XAUUSD, Dec 2022 → Oct 2026, New York time. Choose on 2025, check on 2026 and 2023–24.
  • One basket at a time, iterations all day inside the session. Target = +$20 net on 0.1 lot (price must move $2.20 on the bid, as spread 0.20 and commission $0.70 are paid).
  • Market entries fill at the next 1-minute open. Limit entries (FVG 50%) fill only when price trades through the level by the spread.
  • Exits: EOD = no stop, close what is open at 16:55 New York. HOLD = no stop at all, held overnight until +$20 (swap $2.50 a night, 30 days max). STOP100/200/300 = emergency stop at −$100/−$200/−$300, else EOD. ZRzxn = exact zone recovery, zone $z/10, n legs, hard close when the last leg fails or at 16:55.
  • Entries: break of 5 candles, fair value gap (market or limit at its 50%), VWAP cross, VWAP rejection (dip to VWAP and close back), VWAP stretch reversal, stop-hunt sweep of the 20-candle low/high closing back inside, aggression-candle reversal, EMA20 pullback, RSI 30/70 return, Bollinger re-entry, engulfing at a 20-candle extreme, opening-range break, inside-bar break and a RANDOM coin flip as the control.
  • Filters: none, with the H1 trend, against the H1 trend, VWAP discount (buy under VWAP / sell over), VWAP flow (buy over / sell under).
