Keystone Arc • 6-year research • MNQ + MGC 1-minute bars, Oct 2020 – 2 Oct 2026

The opening range is where the edge is

I tested 5,641 rule variants on your uploaded data. Each rule's settings were chosen on 2020–2023 and then judged on 2024–2026, data they never saw. One family held up on the unseen years: trading MNQ in the direction of the first 10–30 minutes after 09:30, with a wide stop. Everything here is built into the studio and the Live Desk.

The short version

  • Edge found, and it is modest. About $9–18 per trade per MNQ micro after costs, on 1,340+ trades. It still holds when slippage is doubled. Most other ideas (gaps, VWAP, yesterday's high/low, Asia session, calendar, MGC rules) looked good on 2020–2023 and then failed on 2024–2026.
  • Prop pick: ★ OPENING RANGE BREAK • 15M on Lucid Flex 150K: 6 micros to pass, 2 micros once funded, and ask for a payout only once the balance is +$6,000. The same rule run with zero edge loses money, so the profit comes from the edge, not from the account rules.
  • Realistic income: 5 Lucid accounts copying one rule had a median of about $10,000 a year (12-month windows from every start month 2021–2025). 15 of 57 windows lost money (worst −$8,750). That is not $20K a month. Scaling means more accounts at more firms, with the same correlated risk.
  • Personal account: Opening Drive + ORB 30M 1R at 1 micro each has averaged about $540 a month, with a worst drawdown of about $8,000. $4–5K a month at that risk level needs roughly a $200K account. With $27K, the prop route is where your budget works hardest.
The rule, exactly as the studio trades it

★ Opening Range Break • 15M

  1. Skip FOMC days and payrolls (first-Friday) days. Measure the high and low of MNQ from 09:30 to 09:45 New York time.
  2. Place a buy stop one tick above the high and a sell stop one tick below the low. The first to fill wins, and the other is cancelled. Orders that haven't filled by 12:15 are cancelled: no trade today.
  3. Stop: 0.25 × the average 09:30–16:00 range of the last 14 days, measured from the fill. In 2024–26 that was about 60–100 points, a median of $124 per micro and $204 at the 90th percentile.
  4. Target: 4 × the stop. Otherwise, flat at 15:55.
  5. Expect to lose most days. 36% of trades win, but the average win is $271 against an average loss of $132. The longest losing streak was 16 in a row.

What did not work

Most good-looking backtests failed the unseen years

Picking "the best rule so far" each year and trading it the next year lost money on MNQ (−$822 per contract over 2022–26) and on MGC (−$1,764). The same procedure limited to the opening-range family made +$10,857. Gap fades, VWAP stretches, yesterday's high/low, afternoon momentum, weekday and turn-of-month rules, and every MGC rule fell apart after 2023. The one exception was a short-side Asia breakout that only paid off in 2026's gold swings. Your earlier BH lab result (−0.02R over 1,698 setups) fits the same picture.

Fastest
Pro 50K, 4 micros

P6: Lucid Pro has no consistency rule on the evaluation, so it can pass in days (39% pass within ~9 days). Fast but weaker over a year. Use it to get funded quickly, then trade the funded account with ORB15 at 1 micro.

One rule per firm. Lucid bans hedging, including across your own accounts. The Opening Drive and ORB15 can take opposite sides on the same morning, so run one rule on all accounts at a firm. If you want a second rule, put it at a different firm.

Personal account • conservative

Opening Drive + ORB 30M, 1 micro each

Run together, the two rules had a Sharpe of 1.15 from 2021 to 2026. Average about $540 a month (2021 +$5,785, 2022 +$8,450, 2023 +$3,598, 2024 +$7,060, 2025 +$8,137, 2026 YTD +$5,924). Worst drawdown about $8,000, and 29 of 73 months were down. Stop for the month at −$2,500. Add one micro of each only for every +$15,000 the account grows. At this risk level, $4–5K a month needs about eight times the size, so roughly a $200K account.

How to start

From replay to a funded account

  1. Compile build 2026-10-05d. In the Backtest Studio, choose ★ OPENING RANGE BREAK • 15M (TESTED • PROP PICK). ⚙ SETTINGS opens with the tested settings. Set contracts, choose AUTO, then CONFIRM + ▶ PLAY. AUTO places both stop orders, cancels the loser, sets the bracket from the fill and goes flat at 15:55.
  2. Replay 20+ random days with PICK A DAY. Your replay results should match the research trades, which are listed day by day in docs/research/trades_B.csv.
  3. Buy one Lucid Flex 50K (P4). In the Live Desk, click the account, then PLAYBOOK PLAN → P4. The card shows the size, drawdown, target and daily plan.
  4. Trade it exactly: one order pair at 09:45, no overrides. Scale to P1 only after the live results look like the replay.
Limits of this test

What could still go wrong

  • The edge is small compared with its swings. Losing years happen (ORB15: 2023 and 2026 so far), and 2026 is a drawdown right now.
  • Fills come from 1-minute bars. The stop is always assumed to hit first, and targets need a trade through them. Real fast opens can slip more, which is why the +2 ticks column matters.
  • The rule family was found with knowledge of 2024–26 in aggregate. The year-by-year walk-forward (+$10,857) is the cleaner estimate.
  • Firm rules change. Lucid's rules here are from their help centre (see docs/PROP_RULES.md), evaluation prices are assumptions, and other firms were modelled as variants of these rules.
  • Historical research only. The studio and Live Desk place no live orders.
