Trailing vs End-of-Day: who reaches +$2,000 first?

Two accounts, the same instrument, the same contracts, opposite directions, entered at the same minute. Both have a $2,000 maximum drawdown and the goal is +$2,000. TRAILING: the floor is the highest equity (open profit included) minus $2,000, moved every tick. END OF DAY: the floor stays at −$2,000 until the day's close, then the closing equity minus $2,000. Every candle is walked open → low/high → close, with $0.62 commission per contract per side and one tick of slippage on the entry.
