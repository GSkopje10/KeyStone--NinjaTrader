Can a funded account pay the full $2,000 every week?

Rotate or copy?

The same style, with the accounts taking turns (ROTATE) or every account taking every trade together (COPY). A copy group moves as one account, so each COPY figure rests on one path per start date (about 60) instead of 1,200 accounts: its swings are mostly luck.

06 · One batch vs reinvest

$6,000 once: stop after one batch, or roll the payouts into the next

Reinvest: the first $6,000 buys 50 evaluations; when that batch is completely done, its payouts buy the next batch (as many evaluations as the money allows, up to 50). You never add money. Each year starts fresh in January.

07 · The math

The ceiling for a trade with no edge

A market with no edge is a coin weighted only by the bracket: a trade reaches +T before −S with chance S ÷ (T + S). Simulating 40,000 evaluations of that coin under the firm's rules shows how high the bracket alone can take the pass rate.
