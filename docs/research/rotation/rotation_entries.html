Method

  • Real 1-minute bars only. A combined trade enters at the next minute's open + 1 tick per leg; each minute the stop is checked first with both legs at their worst of that minute (conservative), the target only at a minute's close; costs per contract (commission + 1 tick each way), 1 more tick on stops.
  • Rules chosen on Oct 2020 – Dec 2023, judged on Jan 2024 – Oct 2026; shuffle null for the search; walk-forward by year; coin-flip bands for the prop results.
