# EVAL PASS STUDY • 02 Jan 2026 → 02 Oct 2026 (193 trading days)

**Set-up:** batches of 50 evaluations (Lucid Flex 50K rules: pass at +$3,000, $2,000 end-of-day drawdown, 50 % consistency = two days of +$1,500, 2 days minimum). Evaluations only — a passed account stops; a new batch of 50 is bought the day after the last one of the batch has passed or blown. The rotation trades MNQ + MGC together on one account at a time from 18:00 to 16:45 (New York), a trade = +$1,500 target (after the $1.24 / micro costs) or its stop, then the next account. Every price is a real 1-minute bar; the stop is checked with both legs at their worst first. The 📡 indicator was learned on 2025 only and never saw 2026.

Pass % = passed ÷ (passed + failed); failed = blown at the drawdown floor or left too close to it to carry another trade (a quarter of the stop). An account that makes +$1,500 in a day is locked until the next day (the most a day can add). Cost = $120 an evaluation.
