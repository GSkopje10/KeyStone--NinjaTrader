01 · Your plan

Pick your pool and see what to expect

5 accounts = one firm. 10 = two firms or two names. 25 = 5 firms × 5 accounts on your and your wife's names, rotated as one pool. A new set is bought once every account of the last set has passed or failed.

    09 · Method

    How these numbers were made

    Every trade is replayed on real 1-minute MNQ and MGC bars from 2 Jan to 2 Oct 2026 (193 trading days). The rotation trades MNQ + MGC together on one account at a time, 18:00 to 16:45 New York; a trade ends at +$1,500 (after costs) or its stop, then the next account takes its turn. The stop is checked with both legs at their worst first. Rules follow Lucid Flex 50K: pass at +$3,000, $2,000 end-of-day drawdown, 50 % consistency (two days of +$1,500), $120 an evaluation. An account that makes +$1,500 in a day is locked until the next day. Failed = blown at the floor, or left too close to it to carry another trade. The 📡 indicator was learned on 2025 only and never saw 2026. Past results don't guarantee future ones.
