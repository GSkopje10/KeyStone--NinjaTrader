Why frequent can win here. A blown evaluation costs its fee, a passed one can pay $1,000–2,000 several times. So the plan does not need a high win rate — it needs a good chance per evaluation of reaching the payouts, and enough setups to give every account its turn. The score below is only that: payouts ÷ fees. 875 combinations were tried, so the best one is partly luck — the pick is chosen on 2020–23 and shown on 2024–26 it never saw.
