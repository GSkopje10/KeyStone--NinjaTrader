How to read the trailing drawdown here. END OF DAY (Lucid, the 25K): only the closing balance moves the floor, so a trade that was +$1,000 open and closed at +$200 lifts the floor by $200. INTRADAY (Take Profit Trader funded): the best open profit moves it, so the same trade lifts it by $1,000 — a winner that gives back can leave you closer to the floor than the balance shows. Both stop trailing at the lock level (the start balance or +$100); payouts only come from above the buffer.
