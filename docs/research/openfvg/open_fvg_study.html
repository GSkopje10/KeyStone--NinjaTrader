The rule. From 09:30 New York, 5-minute candles. The first FVG (candle 3's low above candle 1's high = bullish, candle 3's high below candle 1's low = bearish) decides the side. If the next candle makes another FVG the same way, it joins the zone (from the lowest to the highest edge) and the order moves to 50% of the whole zone. A BUY limit (bullish) or SELL limit (bearish) waits there; stop −$2,000 (100 pts) and target +$1,000 (50 pts) on 10 MNQ. Not filled by 15:55 = no trade; open at 15:55 = flat. Fills: the limit price (or the open when a minute opens through it); the fill minute can only stop out; after that the stop is checked before the target. Costs $1.74 a contract.
