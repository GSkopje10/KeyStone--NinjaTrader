4 • What this means for the first 50 × 50K

  • Trade the evaluation and the funded account differently. Best found: evaluation +$2,000 / −$1,500 @ 10%, funded +$2,000 / −$1,500 @ 10% — 34.0% pass, 27.9% of funded accounts paid, $1,149 per funded account.
  • ROTATE before COPY for the first money. Rotating spreads the 50 accounts over different days: the risk of no payout at all is 0.7%. COPY puts all 50 on the same trade — the whole $6,000 rides on one account's luck per batch (net $2,849,253 over 6 years, but deeper drawdowns: −$108,000).
  • Filters: keep only those marked ✔ in section 3 (better on days they never saw); one good half is luck.
  • Request every payout the first day it is allowed (the simulation does) and score only payouts − fees.
