Finding 1

After a bearish or bullish first candle, did the price keep going?

Measured from the candle's close at 09:35 to the close of each later time. "Kept going" means the price was further in the candle's direction. The grey tick is the same measure on every other day: a bear candle only tells you something if it beats how often any day fell by then.

Finding 2

Which came first: X points with the candle, or X points against it?

From the 09:35 close, the first minute that touched +X or −X decides it. Minutes that touched both are left out of the share (shown separately). A coin gives 50%.

Finding 3

How far did it go each way?

The biggest move in the candle's direction and against it after 09:35, in points. Quartiles show the typical day; the average is pulled up by big days.

Finding 4

Does it change with the year, the candle's size or the context?

Share of days that kept going, and the average points, at 10:00, 11:00 and the 16:00 close. Highlighted cells have p < 0.05 against a coin. With this many cells, about 1 in 20 lights up by chance.

Finding 5

Can you trade it?

Every combination of follow / fade × 11 day filters × market or break entry × 9 stops × 10 targets × 5 exit times, on one MNQ micro with $1.50 commission and 1 tick of slippage per side. Chosen on Oct 2020–2023, judged on 2024–2026.

The best by luck

Shuffle which days were bull and which were bear, run the same search again, keep the best t-score. 200 shuffles. If the real best does not stand out from these, the search found noise.

Walk-forward

Each year, trade the version that was best on all earlier years. Nothing is chosen with hindsight.

Finding 7

Prop firms: rotation, copy, or one account

Each trade runs through the same prop engine as the studio's PROP SIM, with the firm's rules: target, trailing drawdown checked at the trade's worst point, consistency, minimum days, payouts and split. Five accounts in ROTATION take the day's trade in turn. COPY 5 puts the same trade on all five. A dead account (blown or retired) is replaced by a new purchase. Net = payouts kept − evaluations and activations bought.

Lead vs coin flips on the same days

The lead's days and bracket with a coin choosing buy or sell, 200 times through the same prop rules. "Beats" is the share of coin runs it beat. Above 95% would be strong; 80% happens by luck one time in five.
