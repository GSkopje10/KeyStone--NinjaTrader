5 • How this was tested

  • Entry: market at the session's open minute (FOLLOW / FADE: after the first 5 minutes, the side each instrument moved). Exit: the first minute the combined P/L reaches the target (at the target) or the stop (at the stop — checked first within a minute, both legs at their worst), else flat at 16:44.
  • The stop is the room left above the floor, at most $2,000 — the plan of risking the drawdown for a small daily gain. With less room left, a loss blows the account.
  • COPY: every account takes the same trade, so they pass and blow together. SPREAD: each account its own random sides each day (seeded). SPLIT: half the accounts buy both, half sell both.
  • PASS WITH FVG RETRACE: during the evaluation each account takes every FVG RETRACE trade of the day on 10 MNQ (09:30–15:00, +$1,500 / −$1,500, as the app trades it); funded, the daily target plan.
  • A blown or retired account is replaced by a new evaluation the next day ($120). No activation fee, no payout denials, no slippage beyond the costs — real results will be worse.
