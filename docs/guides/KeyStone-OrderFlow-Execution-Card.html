The 3 setups

▲ BUY

Buy zone holds

Sellers attack a buy zone / VAL and fail.

If
Price comes back DOWN into a ▲ BUY ZONE or VAL, sellers hit it (coral Δ) and it won't break.
Then
🧱 absorption or ⇅ divergence, a candle closes back above, Δ teal, IN CONTROL → BUYERS.
Entry
TAKE on the close (or the zone's top edge).
Stop
Just below the zone / the low of the push.
Target
⚡ scalp 10–20 pts ($20–$40 a contract), or 🏃 swing to the POC / next major level (≥ 2 R).
▼ SELL

Sell zone holds

Buyers attack a sell zone / VAH and fail.

If
Price comes back UP into a ▼ SELL ZONE or VAH, buyers lift it (teal Δ) and it won't break.
Then
🧱 absorption or ⇅ divergence, a candle closes back below, Δ coral, IN CONTROL → SELLERS.
Entry
TAKE on the close (or the zone's bottom edge).
Stop
Just above the zone / the high of the push.
Target
⚡ scalp 10–20 pts ($20–$40 a contract), or 🏃 swing to the POC / next major level below (≥ 2 R).
🪤 FLIP

Trapped traders

Your 22:49 trade: sellers trapped at the low.

If
A zone breaks the wrong way and snaps back: heavy selling at the low, a failed big sell print, price back above.
Then
KeyStone flags "🪤 TRAPPED SELLERS → ▲ BUY" (or buyers → ▼ SELL).
Entry
On the close back inside the zone.
Stop
Beyond the trapped traders' extreme.
Target
The trapped side's stops fuel it: POC first, then the opposite zone.

The card's contracts are already worked out and frozen. Never add to a loser. Use the 🏦 account fit line: the smaller of the two numbers wins.

Day limits (suggested — set your own)

  • Max 3 trades a day. The best setups come 1–3 times.
  • Stop after 2 losses in a row. Come back tomorrow.
  • Funded intraday trailing (TPT): risk per trade ≤ 5 % of the buffer (≈ $100 on $1,900). A profit run raises your floor — take the scalp part.
  • End-of-day trailing (Lucid Flex): the floor moves only at the close, so a 🏃 runner fits better.
  • Hit the day's goal → LOCK FOR TODAY.
  • No trades 2 minutes either side of high-impact news (the NEWS lock).
Once you're in

Managing the trade

HOLDIN CONTROL is your side, the battle map is your colour. Do nothing.
⚠ TIGHTENThe flow is fading against you. Move the stop closer, below the last pullback.
⚠ PROTECT THE PROFITIn profit and the other side took over: take part off or lock the stop in profit.
⛔ GET OUTEverything turned: control, delta since entry, absorption against you. Close it.

Drawdown inside the plan is normal: while the price stays on the right side of your stop's level, the worst advice is TIGHTEN. GET OUT needs a candle closing through that level, or everything against you for 90 s. Advice changes slowly (45–90 s) and never in the first minute. KeyStone only suggests; you act with the chart's buttons.

Never

  • Never buy into sellers who are still winning, or sell into buyers who are still winning.
  • Never trade a "raw" signal before its candle closes.
  • Never trade a 🧪 learning setup with real money, only watch it or paper-trade it.
  • Never move the stop further away.
  • Never take more contracts than the card or the account fit shows.

KeyStone order flow is analysis only. Orders go only through your chart's own BUY / SELL / TAKE, which ask first and use your armed account's limits. The day limits above are suggestions to adapt to your accounts' rules.
