The one idea behind all of it

Every trade has a buyer and a seller. Order flow asks one question: who was in a hurry, and did their hurry move the price?

Aggressive buyer

Buys right now at the higher price (the ask). Pushes the price up. Counted as + in the delta.

Aggressive seller

Sells right now at the lower price (the bid). Pushes the price down. Counted as − in the delta.

Patient traders

Wait with limit orders. They don't push; they absorb the push. When a big push fails to move the price, a patient wall is there.

All KeyStone order flow comes from real NinjaTrader trades (the green REAL TICKS • bid/ask badge), never from candles.

On the chart

The chart marks, one by one

The chip in the corner says what each mark means for a trade: bullish, bearish, by colour (teal = bullish, coral = bearish), or context (a level, not a direction). Click any mark on the chart for its numbers.

▤ STACKED▤ STACKEDby colour

Stacked imbalance

What
3 or more prices in a row where one side traded about 3× more than the other. A real push, not noise. "▤×3" means it happened 3 times close together.
Means
Coral = sellers are strong there (bearish). Teal = buyers are strong there (bullish). It often becomes a zone that side defends later.
Trade?
Not on its own. Wait for the price to come back to it (the watch zone) and for that side to defend it again.
🧱🧱reversal hint

Absorption (the wall)

What
One side pushes hard, lots of volume, but the price barely moves. Someone big is quietly taking the other side.
Means
Teal 🧱 = sellers got absorbed, buyers defending → bullish. Coral 🧱 = buyers got absorbed, sellers defending → bearish.
Trade?
Strongest at a zone, VAL / VAH or the day's high / low. Enter after a candle closes holding the wall.
🪤 trapped buyersbearish

Trapped buyers / trapped sellers

What
A crowd bought (or sold) aggressively at a price, then the price went against them. They are now losing.
Means
Trapped buyers will sell to get out → fuel for a move down (bearish). Trapped sellers will buy to get out → fuel up (bullish).
Trade?
Trade against the trapped side while the price stays beyond their zone. Stop on the other side of the trap.
⇅⇅warning

Delta divergence

What
The price makes a new high but the buying behind it is weaker (or a new low with weaker selling).
Means
The move is running out of fuel. Coral ⇅ at a high = bearish warning; teal ⇅ at a low = bullish warning.
Trade?
A warning, not an entry. Combine it with absorption or a zone.
⚡ ●⚡ ●by colour

Big print (bubble)

What
One very large trade. Bigger bubble = bigger trade. Only the 8 biggest of the last 15 minutes are shown.
Means
A big player acted there. Teal = a big buyer, coral = a big seller.
Trade?
If the price holds above a big buy (or below a big sell), that price becomes support (or resistance).
▲ BUY ZONE ✔▼ SELL ZONEby colour

Watch zone

What
A box KeyStone draws where one side showed strength (stacked, absorption, trapped traders). Only the 2 nearest per side are shown. ✔ = a retest was already confirmed there.
Means
BUY ZONE = buyers' ground (support). SELL ZONE = sellers' ground (resistance).
Trade?
BUY when the price comes back DOWN into a buy zone and a candle closes holding it. SELL when it comes back UP into a sell zone and gets rejected.
POCcontext

POC (point of control)

What
The single price where the most contracts traded today. The gold line.
Means
The day's "fair price". The price is often pulled back to it like a magnet.
Trade?
A natural take profit for trades from the edges. Price far from the POC with weak flow often drifts back.
VAHVALcontext

Value area (VAH / VAL)

What
The band where about 70 % of today's volume traded. VAH = its top, VAL = its bottom.
Means
VAL acts like support, VAH like resistance. Breaking out with strong flow = a new move; failing outside and coming back = a move back to the POC.
Trade?
Best places to look for buys (at VAL) and sells (at VAH) when the flow agrees.
PROFILEcontext

Volume profile (the bars on the right)

What
How much traded at each price today. Each row is split: teal = bought, coral = sold.
Means
Long rows (HVN) = prices people accepted; the price slows down there. Short rows (LVN) = prices the market rushed through; the price moves fast across them.
Trade?
Targets go at the next long row; stops go past a short row.
▮▮by colour

Δ / CONTRACTS row (under the candles)

What
One small bar per candle: teal if more was bought aggressively, coral if more was sold. The number is the delta; grey is the total contracts.
Means
A rising candle with a coral bar = buyers are not really behind it (weak). A falling candle with a teal bar = sellers are being absorbed.
Trade?
Use it to confirm: you want the bar's colour to match your direction on the confirming candle.

Putting it together

When to buy, when to sell

A mark alone is never the trade. A setup needs three things: a good location, one side clearly winning there, and a closed candle that confirms it.

▲ BUY

Buy zone retest

The main setup.

You seeA ▲ BUY ZONE below the price (from stacked buyers or absorption).
You wait forThe price to come back down into it.
ConfirmationSelling into the zone gets absorbed 🧱, a candle closes holding the zone, the Δ bar turns teal.
PlanEntry at the zone. Stop loss just below it. Take profit at the POC or the next long profile row.
▼ SELL

Sell zone retest

The mirror.

You seeA ▼ SELL ZONE above the price.
You wait forThe price to come back up into it.
ConfirmationBuying into it gets absorbed, a candle closes rejecting it, the Δ bar turns coral.
PlanEntry at the zone. Stop loss just above it. Take profit at the POC or the next long row below.
▲ / ▼

Absorption at the edge

VAL / VAH, the day's high or low.

You seeA big push into VAL (or VAH) with heavy volume, but the price stops.
You wait for🧱 absorption and the push to fade (often ⇅ divergence too).
ConfirmationA closed candle back inside, IN CONTROL turning to the defending side.
PlanStop loss past the extreme. Take profit at the POC.
🪤

Trapped traders

Trade against the stuck crowd.

You see🪤 trapped buyers above (or trapped sellers below) the price.
You wait forThe price to stay beyond their zone.
ConfirmationThe flow turns against them (IN CONTROL, Δ row).
PlanSELL below trapped buyers / BUY above trapped sellers. Stop loss on the other side of the trap.

Every trade

Your 4-step routine

  1. Where are we?

    At a zone, VAL / VAH, the POC or the day's high / low? In the middle → don't trade.

  2. Who is winning?

    IN CONTROL and the candle's Δ bar. Is someone defending the level?

  3. Wait for the close

    No setup counts until its candle has closed.

  4. Let NOW decide

    Only ✅ is a trade. Check 🔍 WHY if unsure. Size from the card, never bigger.

From your screen

Worked example: "▤ STACKED • for sellers @ 28222.00"

▤ STACKED for sellers 3 selling imbalances 28212.75–28213.50 • delta −401

  1. What happened: sellers hit 3 prices in a row hard, 401 more contracts sold than bought. Bearish pressure right there.
  2. Where: right on top of a ▲ BUY ZONE and the VAL, the bottom of today's value area. Buyers' ground.
  3. So it's a battle: sellers attacking the buyers' zone. It is not a sell signal by itself.
  4. If buyers win: the selling gets absorbed 🧱, a candle closes holding the zone, IN CONTROL turns to BUYERS → ✅ BUY toward the POC 28240.
  5. If sellers win: a candle closes below the zone, the buy zone breaks, and KeyStone marks a new ▼ SELL ZONE there. You then SELL when the price comes back up into it and gets rejected.
  6. Until one side wins: ⏳ WAIT. That is exactly what the NOW tile said.
