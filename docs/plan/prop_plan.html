⚡ The strategy — built for the prop rules

"Two wins pass."Every trade risks $1,500 to make $1,500 (10 MNQ, ±75 points). Two net wins = the $3,000 target → passed. Two losses = the eval is gone ($120). The same trades keep running once funded, and every 5 qualifying days a payout is taken.

Why this and not something new: I searched 14 strategies × 14 strategies (one for the evaluation, one for the funded stage — FVG RETRACE, MOMENTUM, a RANDOM coin flip; brackets from ±$750 to +$2,000/−$1,000; NY open and NY day; rotating and copying) = 392 plans on 6 years. What the data says:

! Stop rules — decided now, not in the moment

1 • Budget without proof$4,000 of fees spent and no payout received yet → STOP buying evals. No refill from the bank. We review together.
2 • Payout deniedA payout is refused or clawed back → stop buying at that firm. Find out why before anything else.
3 • Live worse than the simTwo months in a row more than 30% below the sim on the same days → stop and re-test (fills, slippage, rules).
