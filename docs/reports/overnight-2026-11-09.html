1 · The TPT trailing lock: which plan

The rule: the floor is the peak equity including open profit minus $2,000. The goal is to reach +$2,000 so the floor locks at the start. Each test is one account per start day (every second weekday). Costs are $2 per contract per round trip. Anything open is closed at 16:54 and the race continues the next day, for at most 40 days. Every figure is worst-case: when a stop and a target fall inside the same minute, the stop counts first.

Plan A · fastest

MNQ 10 at 9:30 NY in the 60-minute direction, TP $2,000 (100 pts), stop = the floor.

40.2 % lock · 35.8 % on day 1 · 1 trade

Plan B · best that holds

MNQ 5, ±50 pts ($500 / $500), stay on a win, switch on a stop, non-stop from 9:30.

41.8 % lock · 11 trades · 2 days · 41.8 / 41.9 % by half

Plan C · slower

4 · How to make the lock concept profitable

  • Do the cost maths first. Cost per locked account = (eval fee ÷ eval pass rate) ÷ lock rate. For example, $220 ÷ 25 % ÷ 40 % ≈ $2,200. A locked account has to pay out more than that over its life.
  • The money is after the lock. Once the floor stops at the start balance, trade the slow SAFE plan and withdraw everything above the buffer. The race only buys the lock quickly.
  • Stagger the races. All 5 accounts on the same day in the same direction is a single bet: all lock or all blow. One or two a day lands you closer to the expected 2 of 5.
  • Don't pair long and short accounts. A pair produces exactly one lock about 75 % of the time, but each account still averages 37.5 %, so nothing is gained. Most firms also treat opposite positions across accounts as a rule break.
  • Next step: compare "race then SAFE" against "SAFE from the start" in the Funded Lab's 🔁 BUSINESS RUN, in dollars spent vs withdrawn, before committing your 5 funded accounts.

5 · SpasiX hedge: faster recovery

Bottom line: adding to the winner did not hold up, so it was not built. The real risk is overnight swap on stuck baskets. A new option, 🧹 COMPRESS HEDGES, removes it without changing your market position.

Your export (8 Nov): 23 iterations, all won, net +$254.56. One iteration needed 4 add-ons, went down −$496.73 and was stuck about 4 hours to earn +$1.75. Iteration #24 was stuck around −$140 (BUY 0.32 / SELL 0.40) while the price chopped between 4120 and 4133.

Why basket #24 is stuck

  • Every hedge is sized "the other side's net + 0.08", so the basket's net is always 0.08 lot, which is $8 per $1 move. Each flip across the $5–6 chop band costs about $40–50, so four hedges come to about −$138.
  • The way out is the same 0.08 net recovering it: (138 + 16) ÷ 8 ≈ $19 of clean move. The next hedge (BUY 0.16 @ 4132.73) would cost about $55 more and push that to about $27.
  • A stuck basket is, by definition, a range market. Adding to the winner is a momentum bet at the edge of that range, exactly where the earlier legs were reversed.

The bigger finding: overnight swap

risk for a real account With your broker's swap charged (about −$62 long and +$28 short per lot per night, triple on Wednesday), every variant, your settings included, hits the 60 % hard stop in 6 or 7 of 7 years. Baskets that stay hedged for weeks with 2–4 gross lots pay $40–70 a night. A plain time stop ("exit if stuck 24 h") was tested and made it worse. The fix being tested now is below.

The fix: 🧹 COMPRESS HEDGES

A hedged basket holds BUYs and SELLs that cancel each other out. Closing an equal amount of both leaves your real market position exactly the same. It only stops paying overnight swap and margin on lots that do nothing. The money already won or lost on those lots stays in the iteration, so the TP, breakeven and rescue lines stay exactly where they were.
