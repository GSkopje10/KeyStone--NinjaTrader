3 setups, 30 accounts, 6 years: the rotation loses money

FVG 50% retrace, aggression break and 123 engulfing on 5-minute MNQ, both directions, first setup wins. 15 micros, −$500 / +$1,500. Lucid Flex 50K rules, evaluations and funded accounts running together, a new batch of 30 when the last one ends. Data: 1-minute MNQ bars, 28 Jun 2020 – 2 Oct 2026 (1,716 sessions).

The answer

  • No version makes money over the whole history. The best 6-year result in your NY 9:30–16:00 window is −$917 per 30-account batch; only 1 in 5 batches ends in profit.
  • The setups win 25.8% of trades; you need 26.7% to break even with this bracket after costs. Every main window sits just under that line.
  • The setups do beat random entries a little over 6 years (NY: −$28 vs −$38 a trade), but in the last 12 months in NY they are no better than random.
  • One trade per account beats “stay until done” in almost every case: payouts come about 2–3× sooner and the losses per batch are smaller.
  • The last month and the last week made no payout at all; every batch is still at −$3,600 (the evaluation fees).
  • ASIA (18:00–02:00) is the only window above zero (+$350 / batch over 6 years, +$543 in the last 12 months), but it is negative per trade and was negative before 2025. That is the payout structure, not an edge.

Does the best session hold up?

Ranked on 2020–2024, the best window was NY PM (+$870 a batch). On 2025–2026 it fell to 4th of 6 (−$1,431 a batch). The best window on 2025–2026 was Asia (+$1,359). The winner changes from one period to the next, which is what noise looks like. No window can be picked in advance with confidence.

How the test was run

  1. Setups on 5-minute candles (built from 1-minute bars, closed candles only). FVG: a 3-candle gap, a limit order at 50% of the gap; if price never comes back, no trade. Aggression: a candle closing beyond the last 10 candles' high (low) with a body ≥ 60% of its range and a range ≥ 1.5× the average. 123 engulfing: the KeyStone rule (a run of 2+ candles, then an engulfing close).
  2. One trade at a time across all accounts. The first setup that triggers while flat takes the trade.
  3. Outcome on 1-minute bars. There is no tick data for MNQ in the repository, so a minute that touches both the stop and the target counts as a stop (20 times in 19,356 trades). The stop fills one tick worse; costs are $26.10 a trade.
  4. Lucid Flex 50K, from the KeyStone firm book: $120 evaluation; $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown that locks at +$100; pass at +$3,000 with at least 2 days and no day above 50% of the target; payout after 5 days of $150+, 50% of the profit up to $2,000, a $250 minimum, 90% to you, up to 5 payouts; flat by 16:45. Two wins ($2,948) fall just short of $3,000, so a pass usually needs 3 winning days.
  5. Batches: 30 accounts bought together; the next 30 the session after the last one blows or retires.
