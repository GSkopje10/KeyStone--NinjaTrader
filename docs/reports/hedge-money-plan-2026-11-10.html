Research on 6 years of 1-minute gold (June 2020 – October 2026), the app's own engine, real costs and overnight swap. Nothing here was changed in the app.

Hedge Money Plan

The short answer: no lot size, stop or withdrawal rule makes the hedge automation grow an account over the long run. Every version I tested earns small wins most days, then a basket gets stuck and gives everything back. Running non-stop on the full 6 years, your current DEFENDER wipes the account in about a year. COMPRESS ALWAYS lasts about 1½ years, and the best entry found lasts about 3½ years. Money management can only make the losses slower and smaller. The safest plan below (VWAP-rejection entries on 15-minute candles, a small fixed lot, $8,000 or more) roughly breaks even: the typical start date ends at about 98 % of what it started with, and the worst dip is about 20–30 %. On $431 or $1,000 there is no safe setting, because the broker's smallest lot (0.01) is already too big for the account.

Tested settings: 5 lot sizes × compounding or fixed × basket stops 0/5/10/15/20/30 % × daily loss 0/3/5/10 % × monthly pause 0/10 % × 7 withdrawal rules × 4 balances = 13,440 combinations. Each was replayed from 64 start dates (every month from July 2020 to October 2025) on two price paths. The replay scales the engine's real trades to each lot size. A stop or pause is approximated: after it triggers, the plan sits out until that basket would have ended. That may make stops look a little better than they really are.

The withdrawal rule, in plain words

At the end of each week, if the account is above your starting balance and no basket is open, take out everything above the starting balance. Never add money to rescue a basket.
Honest note: in the tests this changed almost nothing (±1 %). With these strategies the account rarely gets above its starting balance for long, so there is little profit to take out. Withdrawals cannot turn a losing strategy into a winning one. They only protect money that a lucky stretch has already made.

What it looks like over time

These curves start in June 2020 and run to October 2026, and everything is closed at each New Year (as in all the yearly tests). Run without that yearly reset, the losing baskets keep going until the −60 % hard stop, and every version ends far worse.

Everything else that was tested

  • Partial profits / banking winners / trailing / adding from profit (17 versions): none beat COMPRESS ALWAYS on both price paths. The best one (bank 50 % of the net at 60 % of the target) helped on one path and lost more on the other.
  • Your STEP rule (0.01, 0.02, 0.03 … per hedge, target growing per hedge): failed everywhere. In 3,360 simulated combinations, none beat the current hedging. In the full engine it finished the account in 2–7 of 7 years at every size. At $431 it was finished every year.
  • Higher timeframes (M5 / M15 / M30 / H1): slower candles lose less (M30 and H1 lost about a tenth of what M1 lost on unseen years), but random-direction entries on the same timeframes did just as well or better. The gain comes from trading less often, not from better signals.
  • Multi-strategy ensemble (engulfing, FVG 50 %, aggression break and reversal, VWAP rejection; votes 1/2/3; hedges on opposite signals): no combination earned more than $1,700 on 2020–23. Random-direction copies scored about the same. In the full engine the engulfing + signal-hedge versions lost $5,900–11,600.
  • Big combinatorial search (2,000 combinations of entries, hedge triggers, sizes, exits and add-ons, plus 2,000 coin-flip controls): the best 2020–23 result (+$47,000) turned into −$21,000 on 2024–26. The few that held up out of sample did just as well with coin-flip entries. That is luck plus the 2024–26 gold trend, not an edge.
  • Recovery trades on setups (VWAP reclaim, bands, FVG, EMA pullback, day break, open momentum, trend 70+): each one hits its target before its stop no more often than a coin flip (40–42 % after costs). As the engine's recovery rule, every one wiped 7 of 7 years, exactly like random direction.
  • Hard basket stops, flip caps, box rules, freezing at −k × target: they turn the hidden losses into real ones: −$19,000 to −$53,000.

Why: 1-minute gold has no direction edge at these horizons once the spread and commission are paid, and the swap on long-held baskets is the biggest single cost. The core flow wins small and often only because the losing basket is kept open. Sooner or later one does not come back.
