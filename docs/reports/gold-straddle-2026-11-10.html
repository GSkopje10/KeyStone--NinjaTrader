Gold Straddle Study

XAUUSD · BUY + SELL at the same moment, same lots · 1-minute gold MGC futures (Jun 2020 – 2 Oct 2026, used as XAUUSD bid) + your XAUUSD M1 export (5–9 Nov 2026) · 34,320 configurations × 2 intrabar orders · research only, nothing was changed in the app

No — a gold straddle does not have an edge. Not one of 34,320 variants made money in 2020–23, at either intrabar order, and the best of them lost again in 2024–26.

Opening a BUY and a SELL together costs about $84 per lot up front (two $0.35 spreads = $70 + two $7 commissions = $14). Before costs, the exit rules earned on average about $0 per lot (median −$1 pessimistic / $1 optimistic in 2020–23). So the result is simply the cost: the median variant lost −$87 per lot per straddle (≈ −$1 per straddle at 0.01 lot).

The variant that looked best on 2020–23 (19:00|$5|HOLD bankall back0 cont0 bs4|24h: open at 19:00 New York, bank the winner at +$5, hold the loser until the price returns to the start, basket stop −$20) still lost −$21 per lot per straddle there, then lost −$98 per straddle in the untouched 2024–26 test — worse than the same rules at a random time of day (median −$75).

The app's 9:30 NAS100 preset, moved unchanged to gold (X = ½ of yesterday's 9:30–16:00 range, TP 3X, breakeven stop, flat 16:00), lost −$69 per lot per straddle in 2020–23 and −$43 in 2024–26. 2026 alone was positive (gold's big trending year) — that is the regime, not an edge: every earlier year lost.

Do not run a gold straddle with real money. If you want to keep studying it, keep it on DEMO / BACKTEST.

The math, plainly

  • At the moment both legs are open the pair is frozen. If gold rises $1 the BUY makes $100 per lot and the SELL loses $100. The pair total stays at exactly −(2 spreads + 2 commissions) = −$84 per lot (−$0.84 at 0.01 lot) no matter where the price goes. That is why "close both when the PAIR total reaches +target" can never fire unless a leg is closed first.
  • Every management rule turns the straddle into an ordinary one-direction trade. Cutting the SELL at +X leaves you long from that moment: cut the loser at −X, winner to +3X, stop at breakeven is exactly a buy-stop / sell-stop breakout entered X away, with a stop of X and a target of 3X — but paying the spread and commission twice. Bank the winner and hold the loser is exactly a fade (a counter-trend trade) entered X away. Bank & re-hedge is a grid: many small wins in a range, one large loss in a trend.
  • So a straddle can only make money if gold has a repeatable pattern after a move of X — momentum (breakout rules) or mean reversion (hold-loser, ladder) — strong enough to beat $84 per lot. On 1-minute gold 2020–26 the edge before costs was ≈ $0 (median −$1 / $1 per lot in 2020–23, −$5 / $6 in 2024–26, pessimistic / optimistic). Choosing when to stop (at +X, +3X, a trail, the price coming back) cannot create money on a price with no pattern: it only reshapes the results — high win rate with rare big losses (hold-loser, ladder: 74–79% winners) or low win rate with rare big wins (breakout: 15–32% winners) — with the same average.
  • Swap makes holding worse: the BUY pays −$62.4 and the SELL earns +$28.1 per lot per night (−$34.3 per lot per night while both are open, ×3 on Wednesday nights). The 24-hour variants paid about $6–11 per lot per straddle in swap on top.
  • Lot size only scales the loss. With a negative average, a larger lot or compounding makes the account die faster; a daily-loss stop or a basket stop changes the shape, not the sign.

How it was tested

  • Data: 2,036,559 one-minute MGC bars (Jun 2020 – 2 Oct 2026; Aug–Sep 2020 missing), New York time, close-stamped (shifted to the bar open). Treated as the XAUUSD BID; ASK = BID + $0.35.
  • Costs (the app's XAUUSD model): spread $0.35 in every fill, commission $7 per lot round trip per leg (re-opened ladder legs pay again), swap −$62.4 long / +$28.1 short per lot per night at 17:00 New York (×3 Wednesday), 1:500 margin. Results are $ per 1 lot of each leg (divide by 100 for 0.01 lot).
  • Engine: a purpose-built event simulator (scratchpad straddle/sim.cs), not the SpasiX engine; the rules mirror HedgeAuto.Straddle: triggers at mid = start ± X, the loser cut at market, the winner's stop at its own entry, target +R·X or a trail of X; stops fill at their level or at the open of a gap beyond it. Hand-checked on 5 Mar 2024 (09:30, X = $3 and $5: cut, breakeven, hold-loser, ladder and half-bank fills match the candles to the cent).
  • Intrabar order: every candle is replayed both ways (open → high → low → close and open → low → high → close); "pessimistic" keeps the path worse for the account at the candle's close, "optimistic" the better one.
  • Grid (34,320): 30 entries (every hour 00–16 and 18–23, 08:30, 09:30; compression = range of the last 30 / 60 min < 0.5 / 0.8 × the H1 ATR(14), up to 3 a day; one random-time entry) × 13 X ($2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20; 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 × H1 ATR; 0.25 / 0.5 × yesterday's 9:30–16:00 range) × 22 managements (cut-the-loser with TP 1X / 2X / 3X / trail, each with or without half banked; hold-loser: bank all / half, come back to the start / X/2, continuation stop 2X or none + −4X basket stop; ladder: pair target 0.5X / 1X / 2X, basket stop −3X or none) × 4 time stops (2 h, 4 h, 16:55, 24 h with one rollover). One straddle at a time; no entries on weekends.
  • Selection discipline: ranked only on Jun 2020 – Dec 2023 (pessimistic, ≥ 300 straddles, by t-score); 2024 – Oct 2026 never looked at for the choice. Controls: the same rules at a random minute 01:00–15:00 New York, 30 seeds. Total tried: 34,320 configurations × 2 orders + 90 random controls.
  • Not modelled: wider spreads at the 18:00 reopen and around news (they would make every result worse), slippage beyond the gap rule, broker-specific hedged margin.
