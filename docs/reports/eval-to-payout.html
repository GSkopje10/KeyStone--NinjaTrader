Eval → Funded → Payouts

Batches of 30 × 50K evaluations (10 Lucid, 10 Tradeify, 10 TPT, $120 each), a new batch on every weekday from July 2020 to 2026-10-02, real 1-minute MNQ and MGC data. Costs: $1 a contract round trip plus 1 tick of slippage each side. Every passed Lucid / Tradeify account is traded funded until it blows or reaches 5 payouts; TPT funded accounts are parked. At most 5 funded accounts per firm; the rest wait on the bench.

1 · The one model for the first 30 accounts

The simple story (30 accounts, then reinvest). Full detail in section 9.
  1. Week 1: buy 30 evals ($3,600): 10 Lucid, 10 Tradeify, 10 TPT. Trade them with the ★ staggered eval plan. About 7–8 pass (2–3 per firm).
  2. Weeks 2–4: the passed accounts become funded (at most 5 per firm, the rest wait on the bench). Each funded account trades ★ MAX IN 5 at its own time and its own side, so they don't all win or lose together. The first payouts come about 3 weeks after the purchase. On average a batch of 30 pays about $5,500. 1 batch in 4 pays nothing, and 6 in 10 pay back their $3,600 or more.
  3. Every week: buy the next 30 evals from your starting money and the payouts. The funded accounts keep trading for payouts 2, 3 and more, and new passed accounts fill blown ones.
  4. Grow, then take home: reinvest 100% until you buy 90 evals a week. From then on keep 3 weeks of evals in reserve ($32,400) and take home everything above it.
  5. Start money: $10,800 (3 weeks of evals). With only $3,600, 6 runs in 10 die before the payouts start flowing. With $10,800, 1 in 4 do.

EVAL · ★ STAGGERED ALL AT ONCE

  1. Every trading day, all alive accounts trade once. The 30 are spread over 5 entry times: 2:00 · 8:30 · 9:30 · 10:00 · 11:00 New York (6 accounts each, 2 per firm).
  2. MNQ 20. The side: one coin flip per firm per entry time (never opposite positions inside one firm at the same time).
  3. Take profit +$1,500 after costs (limit). No stop: the account can use its full room. One trade a day, so never more than +$1,500 a day.
  4. Pass at +$3,000 over 2 days (TPT: 3 days, the 3rd a tiny trade). A blown account stops.

FUNDED · ★ LUCID / TRADEIFY

  1. From the day after passing (if the firm has fewer than 5 funded; else the account waits on the bench).
  2. 9:30 New York, MNQ 4. Side = Nasdaq's direction from 3:00 to 9:30 (London continuation).
  3. Take profit +$1,500, stop −$500, one trade a day.
  4. On the 5th day of at least +$150 since the last payout: withdraw 50% of the profit (capped $2,000 Lucid / $2,500 Tradeify), you keep 90%. Repeat up to 5 payouts.
  5. TPT funded accounts: parked (no trading) until a TPT plan is proven.

7 · How to read it, and what to check

  • Simulated on 1-minute closes; a trade still open at the session end closes at the market. One trade per account per day.
  • Eval rules as you gave them (EOD trailing $2,000, +$3,000, best day ≤ 50%, 2 days; TPT 3). Funded rules as you gave them; the drawdown lock at +$100, the $250 minimum payout and 5 payouts maximum are assumptions.
  • Not modelled: activation / reset / monthly fees, daily loss limits, funded consistency rules, news restrictions, the payout waiting time. Check each firm's current rules page before buying.
  • Recent periods are cut off by the end of the data (2026-10-02).
  • Past results don't promise future ones; the edge is small and the results are lumpy.

8 · Max payout in 5 days (Lucid $2,000 · Tradeify $2,500)

Your rule: never request less than the maximum. Lucid pays 50% of the profit capped at $2,000, so the account needs +$4,000; Tradeify needs +$5,000 for $2,500. The best case is 2 eval days + 5 funded days. Every plan below withdraws only the maximum: it trades until the profit allows the cap and 5 days of at least +$150 are done since the last payout. Then half stays and the next cycle needs +$2,000 / +$2,500 more. Funded drawdown: end-of-day trailing $2,000 that locks at +$100. No funded consistency rule (an assumption). Data: one funded account started every 2nd trading day, July 2020 → October 2026, 2,688 + 256 plans.

★ NEW PRESET · MAX IN 5 (Lucid and Tradeify, the same rules)

  1. 9:30 New York, MNQ 6. Side = Nasdaq's direction from 3:00 to 9:30 (London continuation).
  2. PUSH days (until the profit allows the maximum): take profit = the profit still missing, at most +$2,500, with a −$500 stop. One trade a day.
  3. QUALIFY days (once +$4,000 / +$5,000 is there but fewer than 5 qualifying days): MNQ 2, take profit +$200, stop −$600, up to 3 tries a day, just to bank the day's +$150.
  4. Payout on the day both are true: Lucid $2,000 (you get $1,800), Tradeify $2,500 (you get $2,250). Then the same again, up to 5 payouts.
  5. No size increase after the floor locks: it was tested and made less (see the table).

  • His entries are not the edge. Continuation of the opening candle, reversion to the open and break of structure all land at 52–57% for +$1,500 before −$2,000, the same as a random side at the right time (2:00: 55.9%). Their own-rule results are about 0R per trade (−0.08 to +0.06).

Recommended steps

  1. Step 1 · Start money $10,800 (3 weeks of 30 evals). The first 30: 10 Lucid, 10 Tradeify, 10 TPT, with the ★ staggered eval plan.
  2. Step 2 · Funded: ★ MAX IN 5 for Lucid / Tradeify, and the TPT one-shot lock then the post-lock plan. Each account at its own time and side (2:00 / 8:30 / 9:30 / 10:00 / 11:00). Never put everyone on one side.

  4. Step 4 · At 90 evals a week: keep 3 weeks of evals ($32,400) as the reserve and take home everything above it.
  5. Step 5 · Scale with firms, not contract size: about 5 funded per firm, so add firms as the count grows (≈ 7–11 firms for $40–80k a month).
  6. Step 6 · Test the London-open reversion (3:00, 0.25%) on a few accounts before using it widely.

Why the user's +$800 to +$1,000 a day doesn't work as a rule.
  • With $2,000 of room, every day that aims for +$800 to +$1,000 risks a loss big enough to blow the account in 2–3 bad days.
  • Scalping +$400 against −$1,000 or −$1,500 needs a win rate above 70–80% just to break even. Without an edge, that becomes 89–93% of accounts blown and $90–290 per account.
  • The day plans (+$800 / −$200, +$1,000 / −$400) stop out too often: the average per account is under $500.
  • Bigger contracts (15–20 per instrument) and MNQ + MGC together add risk, not edge: MNQ 10 alone was the best size.
What raises the money is more funded accounts at once (it scales almost linearly) and enough evals to replace the blown ones — not bigger targets per account.

The $3K starter plan, step by step
  1. Wait for a discount. Buy 50K evals only at about $40–60 (60–70% off). At $120 with $3,000, don't start: the odds of going broke are 1 in 2 or worse.
