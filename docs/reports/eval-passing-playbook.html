Eval Passing Playbook

Everything tested in this project for passing 50K evaluations (end-of-day trailing $2,000, +$3,000 target, best day ≤ 50%), on real 1-minute MNQ and MGC data from July 2020 to October 2026. Costs: $1 a contract round trip plus 1 tick of slippage each side, $120 per eval. Every plan is checked separately on 2020–23 and 2024–26. A plan counts only if it held up in both.

The short version: about 1 in 3 evals pass with the best plans, roughly 9 of every 30, at about $380 of eval fees per funded account. Each account needs two +$1,500 days before one −$2,000 loss, and with no real edge that happens about a third of the time. No entry signal, VWAP, trend or breakeven moved it by more than 1–3 points. What does matter: size big enough to hit +$1,500 the same day, the take profit after costs, and spreading the 30 accounts so they don't all win or lose on the same trade.

  1. Pool of 30 evals, ordered firm by firm (TPT → Lucid → Tradeify → … → back).
  2. One account trades at a time, all day (from 18:00). The first trade of the batch is BUY.
  3. MNQ 20 contracts. Take profit = +$1,500 after costs (limit at entry + 37.5 pts + costs). No stop: the account rides to its floor (−$2,000).
  4. When the trade closes, the next account trades the same side (after a win and after a blow-up).
  5. Each account trades once a day. After +$1,500 it waits for the next day. It still has its full $2,000 of room (the floor moves up to $49,500 with the balance at $51,500), and it needs one more +$1,500.
  6. A batch ends when all 30 have passed or blown. Start a new batch the next session.

Steady: almost every batch gives 6–13 funded accounts. MNQ 30 gives the same rate (30.8%) in 3.3 sessions per batch.

Tested and rejected

Entry confirmations
VWAP side and pullback, opening range 15/30/60, London continuation, gap, first 30 minutes, prior day, 15-minute and short-term trend. Every one gave 55–58% chance of +$1,500 first per trade. Random gives 57%.
Breakeven and profit locks
Breakeven at +$300 to +$1,000, locks like +$500 at +$1,000: −0.2 to −0.5 points of pass rate and 1–2 more days. They turn winners into scratches.
Aggression reversal (BH-style)
864 versions on MNQ and MGC: none made money in both halves after costs.
Everyone on the same trade
The average looks fine, but batches go all-or-nothing. About two in three pass nobody.
Half buy, half sell at once
Cross-account hedging. Most firms ban it, and it didn't raise the pass rate here.
A +$3,000 single trade
Breaks the 50% day rule: the target grows to twice the best day. 19.8% passed.
MGC only, small sizes
Gold rarely moves $1,500 at 3–5 contracts in a day, so 5–15% passed.
Tiny brackets (±$200)
Costs take a large share of each win; the worst funded results (about 21%).

Funded (TPT PRO, intraday trailing $2,000)

  1. No automation on TPT funded: you click every trade. The Eval Lab's flashing button helps here.
  2. One trade a day, MNQ 4 (best, about 32.5% reach the +$2,100 lock) or MNQ 6 + MGC 3.
  3. −$500 / +$1,000 or −$750 / +$750, fixed target, no runners. Open profit you give back still raises an intraday floor (−3.3 points compared with end-of-day).
  4. Stop for the day after the target or after two losses. Scaling out was worse (−0.9).

Running 30 evals: the daily checklist

  1. Build the pool in the Eval Lab: accounts grouped by firm, so the rotation always goes firm by firm.
  2. Check each firm's rules page once: daily loss limit, minimum days, consistency, close time, automation.
  3. Load ★ BEST (or ⚡ FASTEST). Run ⚡ CALCULATE on the last month to see what it would have done.
  4. Trade in firm order. One trade per account per day; the take profit goes in as a limit order.
  5. Let accounts that hit +$1,500 wait for the next day. Count the 2nd win as the pass, and give TPT its extra tiny day.
  6. Move passed accounts to the Funded Lab, and start the next batch when the current one is done.
  7. Expect about 9 passes per 30 (6–13 is normal), about $380 of fees per funded account, and plan the funded stage with ~30% reaching the first payout.
