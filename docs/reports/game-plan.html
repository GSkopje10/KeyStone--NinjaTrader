Prop Firm Rulebook

The newest results of all our testing and the one model to follow without exception. Real 1-minute MNQ and MGC data from July 2020 to October 2026, costs included ($1 a contract round trip plus 1 tick of slippage each side). Every batch below is 30 × 50K evals (10 Lucid, 10 Tradeify, 10 TPT), a new batch started on every weekday: 1,549 batches.

2 · The rulebook (follow it to the letter)

Eval stage: every firm

Every trading day, every alive eval trades once. The accounts are spread over 2:00 · 8:30 · 9:30 · 10:00 · 11:00 AM New York, firm by firm (never two of the same firm in a row).
MNQ 20. One side per firm at each time (a coin per firm per time), so a firm never sees opposite positions.
Take profit +$1,500 after costs, as a limit order at the broker. No stop: the trade rides the $2,000 room. If neither is hit, close at the session end.
Pass = +$3,000 over at least 2 days (TPT: 3 days; the 3rd day is a tiny trade only to activate). Never trade a passed eval again.

Funded stage: Lucid and Tradeify (🛟 SAFE PAYOUT)

MNQ 1. One trade a day at the account's own time (the eval time it had) and its own side. Never opposite sides inside one firm at the same time.
Bracket at the broker: take profit +$800, stop −$300 (after costs). If neither is hit, close at the session end. No adding, no second trade, no moving the stop.
A qualifying day is a day that closes +$150 or more. Losing days don't reset anything; just keep going.
Request the payout the first day it's allowed: 5 qualifying days done and at least $500 profit. Ask for 50% of the profit (Lucid max $2,000, Tradeify max $2,500); you keep 90%. Then start the next cycle the same way.
At most 5 funded per firm. Extra passed accounts wait on the bench and replace a blown or finished one.

Money rules

Keep the budget for 3 batches before you start. A bad stretch is normal; the average only shows over several batches.
Every payout goes back into evals first, until the money spent is covered. Then take your share.
Buy evals only at a discount when you can. The eval price is the biggest lever we found.
TPT is always traded by hand (the firm doesn't allow automation). Lucid and Tradeify can run on AUTO.

Why small size works in funded

With MNQ 1, the $2,000 room is about 1,000 Nasdaq points. An account almost never blows. It just collects +$150 days: a +$150 day needs only about a 75-point move in your direction by the close, which happens often enough. Bigger size reaches the payout sooner when it works, but blows 7–8 accounts in 10 before the first payout.

6 · Check before you buy (the model assumes these)

  1. Payout minimum: the model requests a payout at $500 profit (50% = $250 minimum). If a firm's minimum is higher, or it needs a buffer (for example, balance at least start + $2,100 before the first payout), SAFE PAYOUT takes longer. Tell me the real number and I'll re-run it.
  2. No funded consistency rule is modelled. SAFE days are small and even, so a consistency rule is unlikely to bite, but check it.
  3. Lucid / Tradeify drawdown: end-of-day trailing $2,000, locking at start + $100.
  4. TPT PRO: intraday trailing including open profit until +$2,000; withdrawals of $1,000 at 80% after that.
  5. Not modelled: activation and monthly fees, news restrictions, the time a payout takes to arrive.
  6. Past results don't promise future ones. Nothing here guarantees a payout. The model makes a payout likely (about 6 in 10 funded accounts, about 93 in 100 batches) and keeps losses small.
