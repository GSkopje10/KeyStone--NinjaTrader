Prop Firm Rulebook

The strict plan to follow without changes once the accounts are bought. Every number comes from the realistic lab engine: real 1-minute MNQ highs and lows (a floor or stop is hit the moment price touches it), $1 a contract plus 1 tick of slippage each side, firm rules as modelled, data 2020-10 → 2026-10. The plan was started on every month and run for 6 and 12 months, with 5 different coin sequences each.

1 · Do we have something?

Yes, measured, not guessed. Each eval bought under these rules is worth about $220 to you on average in payouts (95 % range $169 – $276, 15,396 simulated evals). Any eval bought below that price is profitable on average. The edge is not in the entries (they are close to coin flips). It comes from the firms' rules, from many accounts, from taking payouts early and from buying evals cheaply.

2 · The rules (no changes for 3 months)

Evaluations (Lucid, Tradeify, TPT 50K)

One trade per account per day, at 2:00 AM New York (⚡ ALL AT ONCE). The side is one coin per firm (all of a firm's accounts take the same side; never opposite sides inside one firm).
MNQ 20. Take profit +$1,500 after costs (a limit order at the broker). The protective stop sits at the account's floor (it "rides the room"). If neither is hit, close at 4:54 PM.
Pass = +$3,000 over at least 2 days, best day ≤ 50 %. TPT: a tiny trade on the 3rd day. A passed eval is never traded again.

Funded (🛟 SAFE PAYOUT, SPRINT 0)

Lucid / Tradeify: MNQ 1, take profit +$800 / stop −$300, one trade a day at the account's own time. Request the payout the first day it is allowed (5 days of +$150, at least $500 profit).
TPT PRO: MNQ 2, +$800 / −$300. First beat the trailing drawdown (best equity +$2,000, open profit counts; then the floor locks at the start). Then withdraw above the $2,000 buffer (the model takes $1,000 each time the balance is +$3,000). TPT is always traded by hand.
Max 5 funded per firm per person. Extra passes wait on the bench.

Not allowed (tested, they lose)

Scalping or several trades a day per account · bigger funded sizes · ⚡ sprint · retrying after a loss · moving the target or stop · changing times after a bad week · gold instead of MNQ · profit-lock ladders. A rule changes only after a 3-month review (section 5), never mid-month.

Start: 6 accounts (2 Lucid, 2 Tradeify, 2 TPT) on $3,000 if evals cost about $60. At full price ($120) start 6 accounts only with $5,000 – $10,000.
Step up to 15 accounts when cash reaches $5,000 ($10,000 at full price). Step up to 30 (you + wife) at $10,000 ($20,000 at full price).
Every payout goes back into the business until those levels are reached. Only then keep 30 % of each payout as take-home.
Buy only at a discount whenever possible. The eval price is the biggest lever: at $60 instead of $120 the 12-month result roughly doubles.

5 · Stop and review rules

Monthly check (📦 EXPORT FOR CLAUDE): real pass rate, payouts and $ per eval against the model. Pass rates of 20 – 35 % in a month are normal noise.
Pause buying if cash falls below the price of 2 evals with nothing funded or pending. Wait for payouts.
Stop and review (do not tweak) after 3 months in a row below the model's bad-case line, or if a firm changes its rules (payout minimum, buffers, consistency, automation, account limits). Re-run the tests on the new rules before buying again.
Drop a firm only after 3 months of real results clearly below its range in section 1 (Tradeify is the first candidate).

6 · You and your wife

  1. Each person holds at most 5 accounts per firm. KeyStone tracks each account's owner and enforces the limit.
  2. Check each firm's terms before buying. Most firms forbid trading another person's account and can deny payouts for linked accounts (same device, address or identical trades). A denied payout breaks the math. If the terms require it, each person trades their own accounts.
  3. Safer layout: give the two people different entry times (for example 2:00 for you, 2:30 for your wife). Untested, but it costs little and avoids identical trades across two people.
