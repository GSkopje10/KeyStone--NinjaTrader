Prop Firm Game Plan

Everything we tested, in one page. Real 1-minute MNQ and MGC data from July 2020 to October 2026, with costs ($1 a contract round trip plus 1 tick of slippage each side). The details are in eval-to-payout.html.

1 · The game

It's not about being the best trader. It's about getting the most payouts out of the firms. One trade is close to a coin flip, and no entry we tested (about 7,000 plans) changes that by more than a few points. The money comes from the shape of the game: you pay a small ticket (an eval) for a chance at payouts many times bigger. So we win by:
  1. Cheap tickets: a lower cost per eval is the biggest lever we control.
  2. Many tickets, spread out: different times, sides and firms, so they don't all win or lose together.
  3. Rules that fit each firm: payout caps, drawdown type, bench limits.
  4. Patience and a budget: the averages only show up over several batches.

2 · Our rules (the model to stick to)

StageRules
Eval
all firms
  1. Every trading day, every alive account trades once. The 30 are spread over 2:00 · 8:30 · 9:30 · 10:00 · 11:00 New York.
  2. MNQ 20. One side per firm at each time, so never opposite positions inside one firm.
  3. Take profit +$1,500 after costs (a limit order). No stop: ride the full $2,000 room. Never more than +$1,500 in a day.
  4. Pass = +$3,000 over at least 2 days (TPT: 3 days, the 3rd a tiny trade).
Funded
Lucid · Tradeify
  1. ★ MAX IN 5, spread: each account at its own time; one side per firm.
  2. Push days: MNQ 6, take profit = the profit still missing (at most +$2,500), stop −$500, one trade a day.
  3. Qualify days (profit reached, fewer than 5 days of ≥ $150): MNQ 2, +$200 / −$600, up to 3 tries.
  4. Request only the max: Lucid $2,000 → $1,800 to you, Tradeify $2,500 → $2,250. Up to 5 times.
While the bankroll is small, use ⚡ FAST CASH instead: take the first eligible payout (section 5).
Funded
Take Profit Trader
  1. Phase 1, past the trailing drawdown: 9:30, MNQ 10–15, take profit +$2,250, one trade a day until the floor locks.
  2. Phase 2: withdraw $2,000 at a time while keeping the $2,000 buffer (80% → $1,600 to you). Never more than $10,000 profit in a day. Every trade is clicked by hand.
Portfolio
  • Rotate firm by firm (Lucid → Tradeify → TPT → …) so no firm sees a burst of trading.
  • At most 5 funded per firm. Extras wait on the bench and replace a lost one.
  • No new batch until the payouts have covered the money spent, except to keep a reserve.
  • TPT accounts are always manual. The 11-second hold rule applies on funded accounts.

4 · How to win more (in order of impact)

LeverWhyEffect
1 · Buy evals only on discountThe payouts don't depend on the price you paid.At $40 a batch costs $1,200 for the same ~$5,555 → about 4.6× instead of 1.5×. Ruin with $3K drops from 44–80% to about 13%.
2 · More accounts, spread outThe averages only show up across many accounts.Spreading funded accounts over times and sides cut "batch pays nothing" from 63% to 26%.
3 · Pick the firms that pay most per $ of riskPayout cap ÷ drawdown, eval price, min days, rules.Track the return per firm in the labs; add firms whose rules are generous; drop the weak ones (that trader does the same).
4 · The right payout style for your stageSmall bankroll: cash fast. Bigger bankroll: max payouts.⚡ FAST CASH while below ~$3k reserve; ★ MAX IN 5 after (+$1,345 vs $958 per account).
5 · Size: aggressive, not recklessBigger size is faster but not more often.Eval MNQ 20; funded MNQ 6–10. MNQ 15–20 funded, scalping and small brackets all paid less.
6 · Never hedge inside one firmIt can cost every payout at that firm.One side per firm at a time is built into the labs.
7 · Keep a reserveLosing stretches happen (up to 8 batches in a row).Reserve ≈ 3 batches before taking money home.
Don't chase: "a max payout every 5 days on every account" doesn't exist in the data (the best plans manage it in 25–33% of 5-day windows). Chasing it with bigger size or more trades made accounts blow faster.

5 · Starting with $3,000: ⚡ FAST CASH

  1. Only buy evals at a discount (~$40–60). At full price $3K usually runs out first.
  2. Week 1: 25 evals, Lucid + Tradeify (≈ $1,000). Keep $2,000 for weeks 2–3.
  3. Eval: the model above. Funded: MNQ 6, +$1,500 / −$500, one trade a day, each account its own entry; take the first eligible payout.
  4. Each week buy up to 25 more, reinvesting 100% until a $3,000 reserve exists; the rest is yours.
  5. At most 5 funded per firm (bench). Stop if the money drops below 3 evals with nothing pending.

6 · Your TPT account

+$10,000 in about 2.5 weeks happens about 1 time in 10 (9 in 10 blow). A first goal of +$4,000 → withdraw $2,000 ($1,600 to you) happens about 1 time in 4; then go again from there.
  1. Phase 1: 9:30 NY, MNQ 15, side = Nasdaq's direction from 3:00 to 9:30, take profit +$2,250, the trailing floor as stop, one trade a day until the floor locks.
  2. Phase 2: MNQ 10, take profit +$4,000 (capped by what's missing, never more than +$9,500 a day); MNQ 5 when the room is under $2,000.
  3. Withdraw at the goal. Click every trade yourself (TPT funded doesn't allow automation).

7 · Daily routine

  1. Before 2:00 NY: open the 🧪 Eval Lab / 💰 Funded Lab, check the header: who trades at which time, room left, payouts due.
  2. At each entry time: place the trades the lab shows (AUTO where the firm allows it, flashing buttons for TPT).
  3. One trade per account per day; leave take profits as limit orders; don't move them.
  4. After the close: mark passed accounts → funded (bench if a firm already has 5), request due payouts, log blown accounts.
  5. Weekly: compare real results with the model (the labs' stats), buy the next evals only if the budget rule says so.

8 · Risks (read once)

  • No plan guarantees profit; 1 batch in 4 pays nothing and losing stretches happen.
  • Results are simulated on 1-minute data; real fills, fees (activation, monthly), daily loss limits and each firm's fine print can lower them.
  • Firms change rules and prices; check them before every batch.
  • Only use money you can afford to lose.
