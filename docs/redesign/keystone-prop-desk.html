Why it looks like this: the chart takes the whole window, like MT5. Everything about orders lives in one narrow dock on the right. The box at the top of the dock names exactly who a click reaches: one account, or every account in a copy group. There is no separate “separate” switch to remember. Evaluations

Each account is detected from its name and balance. The firm's rules are applied for you. Example accounts.

The flow: buy an eval, connect it in NinjaTrader, and it appears here under its firm with the right target, drawdown type and consistency rule. Each card answers three things: how far to pass (green bar), how far from blowing it (the floor bar turns yellow, then red), and whether today is still allowed (the consistency cap). Once a card reads PASSED, one click moves it to Funded.

Funded

These accounts use the funded rules: trailing drawdown, buffer, payouts. Automation follows each firm's policy. Example accounts.

Trailing drawdown, shown live: the purple line is the floor. It follows your highest balance until it locks at start + $100. After that the account can only lose the buffer above that line. The next payout date and the amount you can safely withdraw are on every card. TPT funded accounts show a hand icon (no automation): the strategy can suggest, but you click.

Copy groups

A group has one leader and its followers. An account belongs to one group only. Switching a group on asks once.

Simplified: no desks or virtual leaders to learn. Pick a group in the Trade dock and every click reaches its members, each with its own limits. Each follower shows a sync dot: green means its position matches the leader's, and yellow means it is off. FIX SYNC brings the off ones back in line, and asks first.
